**THE BILLIONAIRE WEALTH INVSTIGATION**



**PROJECT OVERVIEW**

The Billionaire Wealth Investigation is an SQL data analysis project that explores the world's top richest people. The project focuses on analyzing billionaires' net worth, age, country and industry to uncover patterns in global wealth distribution.  Using SQL, I explored the dataset by writing queries to answer business questions, compare wealth across countries and industries, and identify interesting trends among the world's wealthiest individuals.



**PROJECT OBJECTIVES** 

The objectives of this project are to:

* Identify the wealthiest individuals in the dataset.
* Examine the age distribution of billionaires.
* Analyze billionaire wealth across different countries.
* Identify industries with the highest number of billionaires.
* Compare average and total net worth across countries and industries.

Use SQL aggregate functions and filtering techniques to uncover meaningful insights.



**TOOLS AND TECNOLOGIES** 

* SQL: Used to query, filter, sort, and analyze the dataset.
* SQLITE Online: Used to execute SQL queries and perform the data analysis.
* GitHub: Will be used to publish and share the completed project.



**DATASET DESCRIPTION**

The dataset contains information about 200 of the world's richest billionaires.

The columns used in the analysis include:

* Serial Number
* Rank
* Name
* Age
* Country
* Net Worth
* Industry



**ANALYSIS QUESTIONS**

1\. Retrieve the 20 richest billionaires.

2\. Identify the five youngest billionaires.

3\. Calculate the average age and average net worth.

4\. Identify the five most represented countries.

5\. Calculate the combined net worth of billionaires from each country.

6\. Identify the five industries with the highest number of billionaires.

7\. Find countries with at least five billionaires.

8\. Calculate the total wealth of billionaires younger than 50.

9\. Identify billionaires whose net worth exceeds the overall average net worth.

10.Identify the industry with the highest average net worth.

11.Which industry contributes the most to the combined wealth of the top 200?

12.Which country has the highest average billionaire net worth?
